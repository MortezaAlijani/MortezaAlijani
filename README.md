I work at the intersection of **intelligent sensing, localisation, estimation, signal processing and autonomous systems**, developing model-based and data-driven methods for **visible light positioning and sensing (VLP/S)**, optical wireless sensing, IoT-enabled localisation and sensor fusion.

The question running through all of it: *how much information does a signal carry about where things are?* I work with both **radio-frequency** and **optical** signals.

🎓 **PhD in Electrical Engineering**, Ghent University and imec, 2026<br>
Thesis: *Design of Unmodulated Visible Light Positioning and AI-Enhanced Visible Light Sensing Approaches*<br>
Earlier: MSc in Mechatronics Engineering (University of Trento) and MSc in Electrical Engineering (Communications)

## 🔬 Research Interests

* Visible light positioning and sensing (VLP/S), optical wireless sensing
* Radio-frequency positioning and sensing: GNSS-based cooperative localisation, spectrum sensing
* Localisation, positioning and navigation
* Estimation, sensor fusion and statistical signal processing
* Machine learning for sensing and localisation
* IoT-enabled and autonomous systems
